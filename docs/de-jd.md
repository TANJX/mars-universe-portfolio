## What's Design Engineering?

Design engineers bring "taste" into engineering. They ship high-craft UI while building the systems, tooling, and workflows that help teams move faster and build better products in an AI-native environment. At InstaLILY, design engineers own production UI quality, design systems as infrastructure, and prototyping workflows that turn emerging ideas into shipped experiences.

---

## What You'll Be Doing

### Shape AI-native product experiences
- Design and build customer-facing experiences across chat, voice, copilots, and agentic workflows.
- Turn concepts, prototypes, and product ideas into polished, production-ready interfaces.
- Bring strong front-end craft to interaction design, responsiveness, and implementation detail.

### Build design systems as infrastructure
- Build and evolve a design system in code as shared product infrastructure, across core components, tokens, patterns, and documentation.
- Help make the system machine-consumable, so both humans and AI-assisted tools can generate consistent interfaces from shared foundations.

### Bring taste to what ships
- Bring strong judgment to UI craft and implementation quality, making thoughtful decisions around interaction design, accessibility, performance, and maintainability.
- Help ensure what ships feels clear, cohesive, and intentional, with the discernment to refine details, uphold standards, and know what elevates the product experience.

### Improve tooling and team workflows
- Build internal tools that support teams across engineering, design, and functions like sales, operations, marketing, and recruiting.
- Evaluate and integrate modern AI tools, then turn those learnings into secure, repeatable workflows the broader team can build on.
