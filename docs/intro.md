## Translation Notes

Open calls in each draft. Worth deciding before locking.

### 中文 / Mandarin

- **Display name.** Currently *Mars Tan* in Latin letters across all three locales. If you'd prefer a Chinese name (谭 + given name) in this version specifically, swap in. The switcher could legitimately show *Mars Tan* in EN/JP and the Chinese name only in 中文.
- **"Taste"** rendered as **品味**. Alternatives: 品位 (slightly more formal), 美感 (aesthetic feeling). 品味 is the most common in design contexts.
- **"Design engineering"** rendered as **设计工程**. The literal calque — the term doesn't have wide currency in Chinese tech, where the role is usually called the English *Design Engineer* or 设计工程师. Reading 设计工程 as a *discipline name* will feel slightly novel. Acceptable for a personal site, but worth knowing.
- **"Hold both at once"** rendered as **握住这两端** (grip both ends). Keeps the English's tactile metaphor. Alternative: 同时把握 (more abstract).
- **"Iterate"** rendered as **打磨** (polish, refine) rather than the technical 迭代. Pulls on the craft register from the JD; swap to 迭代 or 持续迭代 if you want the technical word.
- **"Physical goods economy"** rendered as **实体商品经济**. Alternatives: 实体经济 (broader, conflicts with macroeconomic usage), 实物经济 (real-goods economy). Current is the most precise.
- **"USC Iovine and Young Academy"** kept in English as a proper noun. Could prefix with 南加大 (USC) — *南加大 USC Iovine and Young Academy* — to surface the parent institution for Chinese-speaking readers.
- **Simplified vs traditional.** Drafted in simplified. If the audience leans Taiwan or Hong Kong, switch to traditional.

### 日本語 / Japanese

- **Confidence level.** This is a careful but beginner-shaped draft. Expect to rewrite it in your own voice. Use as a structural skeleton, not finished prose.
- **Display name.** *Mars Tan* in Latin letters. Could substitute マーズ・タン in katakana if you want a more localized feel.
- **"Taste"** rendered as **テイスト** (loanword, common in design contexts). Alternative: 美意識 (aesthetic sense) — more elevated, more distinctly Japanese in flavor. Lands harder, costs the direct callback to the English JD's "taste."
- **"Design engineering"** rendered as **デザインエンジニアリング** (loanword, standard in JP tech).
- **"Hold both at once"** rendered as **その両方を同時に担う** (shoulder both at once / take charge of both at once). The English uses *hold*, which would translate literally as 抱える or 握る. 担う lands closer to the work-related sense and reads more naturally.
- **"Iterate" → 磨き続け** (keep polishing). Pulls on the craft register, like 打磨 in the Chinese version. The technical 反復する is available if you want it.
- **"Physical goods economy"** rendered as **フィジカルグッズの経済** (loanword phrase). Alternatives: 物理的な商品の経済 (more native, slightly heavier), 物販エコノミー (marketing-flavored).
- **"Ship"** rendered as **送り出す** in the closing clause (more craft-flavored than リリースする).
- **Sentence length.** The ¶2 sentence is long, mirroring the English. Native Japanese editorial style might break it into two or three sentences. Easy to adjust.
