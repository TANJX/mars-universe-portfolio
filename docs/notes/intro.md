# Intro

## English

**Mars Tan**
Senior Design Engineer at InstaLILY AI.

A good product is both crafted and built, and someone has to hold both at once. I think of design engineering as bringing taste into engineering.

At InstaLILY, I iterate on prototyping workflows that turn emerging ideas into shipped experiences, evolve a design system shared across humans and agents, and ship enterprise-grade agentic workflows for the physical goods economy.
